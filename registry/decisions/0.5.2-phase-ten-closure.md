# Registry 0.5.2 — Phase 10 closure

Registry `0.5.2` closes the remaining final-plan contract and validation gaps
without changing the 28-component topology or dependency graph.

## Decisions

- Every generated component declares a content-addressed revision in
  `sha256:<digest>` form. The digest covers its normalized name, dependencies,
  capabilities, resolved licenses and notice hashes, and artifact declarations
  including canonical source hashes.
- Registry revision remains the release identity; component revision identifies
  the exact contract and bytes of one independently selectable component.
- `update --overwrite` is the only explicit force operation. It may replace only
  ownership scopes in the selected component closure. Untracked conflicts remain
  blocked because the framework has no prior authority over them.
- Repository-local CODEOWNERS rules identify the human reviewer for canonical
  context, registry, installer, runtime, policy, and governance surfaces.
- The installer, project-shape, isolation, and anti-dilution matrices are proven
  through their existing repository-owned test and acceptance owners.

## Preserved boundaries

- All project-authored distributable components remain MIT licensed.
- Objective kernels, schedulers, queues, databases, task envelopes, Codex SDK
  workflows, and long-running agent loops remain outside every component.
- No host product dependency, compatibility schema, model-authored merge,
  publication, deployment, or push is introduced.
